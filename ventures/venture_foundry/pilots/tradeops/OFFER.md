**Business:** independent from Alliance Building & Hazmat Group.  
**Target:** Australian trade and service businesses with small crews, missed enquiries, follow-up backlogs and no desire to administer multiple disconnected tools.  
**Primary goal:** first accepted, paid, profitable customer installation; do not substitute leads, proposed prices or testimonials for actual revenue.

## Product being offered
**JARVIS TradeOps Founding Pilot**: AU$790 initial setup and AU$299/month, both excluding GST, subject to the final written scope and customer agreement. Quote these as *proposed founding rates*, not established sales.

**Pilot scope**
1. Integrate one tested enquiry source such as an existing website form or approved inbound mailbox.
2. Capture genuine enquiries, deduplicate, organise scope and urgency.
3. Create a queue of quote *drafts* and follow-up *drafts* for staff review, not automatic sending.
4. Prepare booking requests, conflict checks and job preparation checklists.
5. Present a simple owner dashboard and weekly service/lead report.
6. Include defined onboarding, acceptance testing and reasonable maintenance for the agreed components.

**Excluded unless separately contracted and successfully tested:** AI telephone receptionist and phone minutes; auto-sending SMS/email; bank logins; bank transfers; final quote approval; contract signing; regulated trade licensing; accounting advice; payment processing; marketing spend; site access and subcontractor commitments; an unlimited AI agent workforce; ServiceM8/Tradify/Xero integration not already authorised.

**Service levels:** Define during scope acceptance. Do not promise same-day activation, 24/7 customer service, ROI, conversion uplift, uptime or unlimited usage before operations substantiate it.

## Commercial plan | first seven days
**Day 0, first revenue attempt:** owner reviews the offer, chooses three genuine warm business contacts or professional network introductions, authorises personalised outreach. Prepare drafts only, never send without specific owner approval. Offer a free ten-minute workflow fit check and a written scope. A paid contract/invoice may be generated only after scope, pricing, delivery timeframe and refund terms are accepted. Never claim a payment until independently reconciled or record it as OWNER_CONFIRMED separately.

**Day 1–2:** conduct discovery on one genuine prospect, identify the existing website/mail/calendar stack, capture one real problem and quantify relevant baseline (enquiries, response time, time to quote), respecting privacy.

**Day 2–4:** configure an isolated pilot tenant using only the agreed integration; use sample records in test and confirm production provenance. Demonstrate enquiry → task → draft reply/quote → human approval → booking preparation. Collect acceptance evidence.

**Day 4–7:** deliver agreed onboarding and a factual results report; request signoff, collect invoice under the customer agreement, reconcile actual payment. Begin subscription only at the agreed service-commencement milestone.

**Weekly thereafter:** compare source-linked enquiries, drafted responses, approvals, bookings and paid work against the pre-pilot baseline. Never convert quotes into banked revenue automatically.

## JARVIS product factories
- **TradeOps factory (first):** ship and support a managed business-automation product; separate tenant records, identity, quotas, prompts and logs. Prioritise one reliable working integration before building large vertical catalogues.
- **Web micro-tools factory (second):** original, useful trade/business calculators and widgets sold by subscription or licence, with trustworthy claims and test coverage.
- **Digital assets factory (third):** original downloadable frames, overlays, GIF/animation assets, graphics and editable templates. Research buyer search intent and licensing on the selected marketplace.
- **Intelligence factory (shared):** verify demand by existing offers, reviews, buyer intent and actual transactions; score ideas for differentiation, feasibility, gross margin and distribution.

**Factory state machine:** discovered → evidence-backed → scoped → approved-to-build → built → tested → owner-approved-to-list → published → genuine order → paid and fulfilled → measured → iterate/retire. All created work is distinct from genuine external sales. Each factory must produce machine-verifiable deliverables and cost attribution.

## SaaS scaling (after first paying pilots)
Build a **hosted, tenant-isolated platform** with limited-scope starter and managed tiers. We retain the platform and orchestration under the venture; customers pay a contractual licence/subscription, with export and termination terms. We do NOT simply fork production JARVIS with Alliance's client records, secrets or vendor tokens, and do not give clients the master platform credentials.

Technical gates before taking multiple tenants:
- AuthN/AuthZ per customer; strict per-tenant database access; secrets vault; backups and recovery.
- No cross-tenant retrieval or prompt contamination; data retention and deletion policies.
- Consent/notice and Australian privacy obligations; permitted AI communication disclosures.
- Idempotent events and audit trails; human review before external communications, quoted commitments or controlled actions.
- Per-tenant model budget, SMS/voice consumption limits and vendor usage pass-through terms.
- Invoice/GST/accounting records, transparent cancellation and refund terms, incident response.
- Existing project's open-source and third-party licences reviewed before commercial use; retain attribution and copyright notices as required.

## Unit economics decision gate
Setup gross margin = setup fee − implementation labour cost − onboarding tool costs.
Monthly contribution = subscription − LLM/provider spend − hosting/database − SMS/voice − platform fees − support/monitoring − refunds.
Target >70% monthly contribution margin after genuine measured usage; if costs exceed target, revise price, scope or delivery. **This target is a decision threshold, not a measured margin.**
